# Consultation: Closed

What Claude may read in this notebook, chosen when it was set up and named in its `CLAUDE.md`. **The notes, and what the user says or shows - nothing else.** The host contract names the domain's particulars - which sources are closed, and why.

## What may be read

- **The notebook itself**, every page of it.
- **What the user says, and what they show** in the session: a screenshot, a pasted passage, a page they attach. Showing it is what brings it in.

**Never the web, a reference work, or Claude's own memory of the domain** - not to answer a question, not to check a fact, not to fill a gap. A gap stays a gap, stated as one.

## Bringing in a source

Only the user brings a source in, by giving it. Claude never goes looking for one, and never suggests where to look.

## Citing

What the user showed is cited as that: the session or occasion it was shown in, in the page's `source:` and on the bullet.

**A source read to answer a question is quoted in the reply, not filed.** It is filed - in `## Known`, cited as above - when the user asks for it to be noted, or gives it as something to record.

## What Claude already knows

**Claude's knowledge of the domain is not a source here.** Where it would answer something the notes do not, the notes do not answer it - say so, and stop. General knowledge outside the domain - how to read a date, what a word means - is fine.
