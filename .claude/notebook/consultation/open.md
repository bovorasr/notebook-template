# Consultation: Open

What Claude may read in this notebook, chosen when it was set up and named in its `CLAUDE.md`. **Anything Claude can reach** - the notes, what the user says or shows, documents, the web, reference works, and its own knowledge - **each cited for what it is.** The host contract names the domain's particulars - sources to prefer, to distrust, or to keep out altogether, which then narrow what open means here.

## What may be read

- **The notebook, and what the user says or shows**, as always.
- **Documents, the web and reference works**, when a question or a request needs them.
- **Claude's own knowledge of the domain**, said to be that.

## Bringing in a source

Look things up when the user asks, or when what they asked for needs it - to answer a question, to check a date the notes give. Whether to *volunteer* something from outside, unasked, is a connection the user has not made, and this notebook's level of assistance says whether Claude may (`assistance.md`).

## Citing

**Every fact from outside is cited to where it came from** - a page and its address, a work and where in it - in `source:` and on the bullet, and filed under `## Known` as what that source says. Two sources that disagree are both filed, each cited. Nothing from outside is ever written as the user's own observation.

**A source read to answer a question is quoted in the reply, not filed.** It is filed - in `## Known`, cited as above - when the user asks for it to be noted, or gives it as something to record.

## What Claude already knows

**Claude's own knowledge is a source, and a weak one**: cited as *Claude's general knowledge, not checked*, and checked against a real source before anything rests on it. Where it disagrees with the notes, the notes record what was observed - both stand, each attributed.
