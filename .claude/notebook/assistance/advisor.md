# Assistance: Advisor

This notebook's level of assistance - how far Claude goes beyond recording - chosen when the notebook was set up and named in its `CLAUDE.md`. **Claude may point out what it sees, in its replies, and records it only when the user asks.** The interpretation stays the user's: what Claude sees is offered, clearly marked as Claude's, never filed as if the user had seen it.

The direction test (`contract.md`) asks whether something would tell the user anything they have not already said or read. **At this level, when the answer is yes, Claude may say it in its reply** - marked as its own inference, with the evidence - but does not write it into the notes unless the user asks.

## Answering

**Answer from what the notes state, and say so.** Where the answer needs a connection the notes do not make, Claude may make it - labelled as its inference, with the pages it rests on: *the notes do not say this, but [[A]] and [[B]] together suggest...* Never present an inference as something the notes say.

## Connections

- **The user owns the interpretation.** Record what they observed and what they made of it, as they gave it - their reading even when it looks wrong, unhedged, and never replaced by Claude's.
- **Claude may point out a connection the user has not made**, in its reply, marked as its own and naming the evidence. Do it at a natural pause - answering a question, or one line after the reply that closes an occasion - not on every capture while the user is entering things in a row, and say it once.
- **Written into the notes only when the user asks**, and then under `## Inferred` (Markers, below) - not `## My reading`, which is the user's own words.
- **Facts about the notebook are always fine**: counts, coverage, what changed, what has no evidence yet.

## Conflicts

**Claude may point out that two readings or two sources disagree**, in its reply, naming both - at the same natural pause as a connection, once. It never decides between them: both stand, each attributed, and which is right is the user's conclusion. **In the notes, each account is filed as its source gave it**, never framed against another - *instead*, *unlike*, *not as X says* belong in the reply, unless the user asks for the conflict to be written down, under `## Inferred`.

## Structure

**Structure is made only on the user's word** (`contract.md` law 2). Claude can be wrong, and structure that is right from one vantage point is useless to a user who does not understand it; and a type, a folder or a page name reads as settled whoever made it (law 3), so it cannot carry an "inferred" label.

**Claude may suggest structure when the user raises structure or asks about it** - a type, a relation, a section that may be pages of its own (`promotion.md`) - with its reasoning and the evidence, and talk it through. The user decides, and invokes `/promote`. Claude does not raise structure where the user has not.

## Projections

**A projection may make connections the notes do not**, each marked as Claude's inference with the pages it rests on, never woven into the account as if the notes said it. A prepared entry may ask about a conflict it sees, saying whose accounts differ.

## Markers

Three kinds of statement, in three sections, never mixed (`contract.md` law 6):

- `## Known` - what a source actually said
- `## My reading` - the user's own conclusions, each keeping its marker
- `## Inferred` - **Claude's inference, recorded on the user's behalf.** Whether the user understands or endorses it is unknown; it is an epistemic status, like the other two, whatever the wording. Each bullet links the evidence it rests on. At this level a bullet goes here only when the user asks for it to be written down. A page gains the section, after `## My reading`, the first time it needs it.

**An inference the user restates in their own words is theirs**, and moves to `My reading`. One they ask to have recorded without that stays `Inferred`.
